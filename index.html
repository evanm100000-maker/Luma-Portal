About Luma Oportal

Portal Overview

Luma Oportal is the official, centralized Operations & Management portal for Luma Roblox Airline. It equips staff members, flight crews, and executive leadership with real-time flight allocations, attendance registers, disciplinary tracking, support desk communications, and performance analytics.

Weekly Flight Quota

Standard staff fulfill a mandatory 3 flights/week quota. Reduced Activity staff fulfill 1 flight/week, and active LOA staff have 0 quota obligations.

Disciplinary Matrix

Maintains C1-C2 warnings, C3 informal sanctions, C4A/B detentions, and C5 suspensions with live countdown account lockouts.

Command & Support Desk

All incident reports and support tickets are reviewed directly by Head Admin Command. High-priority broadcasts deliver critical flight updates across all active crew terminals.
